# Conduct Quotient — Standard 0.5

Conduct Quotient (CQ) is the proposed private, contextual score produced from independently qualifying Net Conduct Score and Verified Conduct Index inputs.

The working display runs from 1.00 to 5.00. The model must account for evidence sufficiency, confidence, recency, disputes, patterns, and context. When evidence is insufficient, disputed, invalid, or irrelevant, the system shows a status rather than a score.
