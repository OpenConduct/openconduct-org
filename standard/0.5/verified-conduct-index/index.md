# Verified Conduct Index — Standard 0.5

Verified Conduct Index (VCI) is the proposed operational-record input for eligible responsibilities a customer accepted and could control.

An approved record must establish source, identity, responsibility, evidence, and uniqueness. Negative records remain pending through notice, cure, correction, and dispute processes. Events outside the customer's reasonable control do not count.
