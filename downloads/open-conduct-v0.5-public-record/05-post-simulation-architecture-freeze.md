## 1. Purpose of this freeze

The Conduct idea has completed its broad structural challenge. This document freezes the architecture that survived so the next phase can tune formulas, thresholds, recency, confidence, context mappings, interfaces, and pilot parameters without quietly reopening the system's purpose, rights, or trust boundaries.

This is not a production specification, legal approval, validated measurement system, pilot authorization, or claim that CQ works in the field. It is the stable research container inside which those questions can now be tested.

The freeze creates three categories:

1. **Frozen structural boundaries:** the project may not tune around them.
2. **Surviving candidates:** lead choices that research may confirm, refine, or reject without changing the project's identity.
3. **Open implementation and empirical questions:** decisions that require prototypes, human research, field evidence, professional review, or a concrete pilot.

## 3. One-sentence architecture

The Conduct ecosystem is a voluntary, upside-only, consumer-controlled system that combines protected human experience and responsibility-bounded operational evidence into contextual dimensions and purpose-bound positive proofs, while preserving baseline service, distributed custody, correction, independent accountability, and zero default transfer across contexts.

## 4. Frozen structural boundaries

### 4.1 Purpose and baseline

- The system recognizes and may reward constructive conduct; it does not rank human worth.
- Participation, history sufficiency, proof presentation, withdrawal, or nonparticipation may not reduce ordinary baseline service.
- CQ is not a fraud, safety, credit, employment, insurance, housing, eligibility, law-enforcement, blacklist, or rights-restriction system.
- The launch direction is upside-only. Negative external proof and verifier-visible nonpresentation state are prohibited.
- Consumers retain notice, access, explanation, challenge, correction, withdrawal, selective disclosure, and remedy rights appropriate to each stage.

### 4.2 NCS human-experience component

- NCS represents an eligible human worker's willingness to serve the consumer again based only on how the consumer interacted with people.
- It is not a general customer-value, profitability, loyalty, spending, fraud, safety, rule-compliance, or manager-satisfaction score.
- The worker must have enough relevant direct interaction to answer; “not enough interaction to say” remains outside scoring.
- Worker responses remain protected from customer identification, manager scripting, performance use, quota use, retaliation, and routine individual disclosure.
- Human experience is not replaced by machine inference, manager opinion, legacy customer ratings, or business outcome data.

### 4.3 VCI responsibility-evidence component

- VCI is a governed representation of verified responsibility evidence, not objective truth or a mathematical correction of human bias.
- Only approved event classes and finalized eligible lifecycle states may contribute.
- Source completeness, attribution, consumer control, business causation, accommodations, evidence quality, correction, concentration, and missingness remain non-compensating gates.
- Business-caused failure, ambiguous responsibility, unresolved state, and omitted context cannot become consumer fault.
- Operational evidence remains separately inspectable from NCS.

### 4.4 CQ synthesis and presentation

- The lead CQ state is two contextual dimensions—NCS and VCI—rather than one universally compensating scalar.
- One component may not silently erase or compensate for failure in the other.
- Purpose-bound proofs lead external business presentation.
- Overall CQ is not a specified production capability and may be permanently rejected.
- The consumer sees understandable meaning, sufficiency, context, and explanation rather than unjustified precision.

### 4.5 Context and portability

- Every unapproved source-to-target mapping begins and remains at zero.
- Portability exists only through a versioned, exact, revocable source–target–purpose profile.
- NCS and VCI mappings are validated separately.
- Company, industry, platform, corporate family, database, brand, or implementation similarity does not itself establish relevance or independence.
- Ordinary verifier proof reveals no named contributing company, competitor, source count, event volume, composition, or source-specific result.

### 4.6 Proof, permission, and benefit

- The consumer initiates the lead initial proof journey and authorizes each initial use.
- A verifier receives only an approved positive proof and minimum fulfillment information.
- Insufficient history, ineligibility, decline, failure, expiry, withdrawal, and nonparticipation remain indistinguishable from baseline to customer-treatment systems.
- Proof validation and benefit fulfillment are separate lifecycle states with consumer-visible completion receipts.
- Initial benefits are modest but meaningful, reversible, nonessential, non-rights, and genuinely non-positional through additional capacity.
- Scarce, queue-changing, rights-related, punitive, or coercive benefits are excluded from the initial profile.

### 4.7 Identity, privacy, custody, and recovery

- Identity assurance is proportional to the action and benefit; maximum identity strength is not the default.
- Authentication, identity proofing, interaction attribution, holder binding, authorization, duplicate resistance, and recovery remain separate functions.
- No universal public consumer identifier or public personal conduct profile exists.
- The lead custody direction composes functional separation, consumer-local computation where feasible, accessible custodial alternatives, and privacy-protected federated retrieval.
- Central reconstructable cross-company raw-history custody is rejected.
- Source custodians may not learn the verifier, purpose, benefit, or unsuccessful presentation activity through retrieval.
- Recovery safely returns to baseline when history cannot be restored; device or credential loss does not produce an adverse signal.

### 4.8 Governance, challenge, and remedy

- A future OCA governs open standards, certification, accountability, rights, and unique network analysis; it does not automatically operate every service.
- Consumers, workers, businesses, technical experts, and public-interest stakeholders require meaningful power with conflict and capture controls.
- Challenges are triaged by issue type and risk; disagreement alone does not create endless bilateral argument.
- Individuals may contest source facts, attribution, lifecycle, active effects, calculation, disclosure, and company-specific application through a reviewable process.
- Urgent harm receives containment and independent escalation; ordinary challenge, systemic investigation, appeal, correction, and remedy remain distinguishable.
- Businesses and providers receive notice, evidence sufficient to respond, neutral review, proportional decisions, and independent appeal.

### 4.9 Reports and institutional economics

- OCA's unique analytical asset is a governed network lens and query authority—not general custody of raw NCS, VCI, consumer, worker, transaction, or proof histories.
- Report families use the lightest validated mix of secure aggregation, fixed federated queries, restricted aggregates, or controlled research environments.
- Open-ended member queries and named-competitor or person-level intelligence are rejected.
- Complete governed contribution, privacy thresholds, cumulative query budgets, corrections, method manifests, public-interest escalation, and independent interpretation remain mandatory.
- Paid depth buys analysis, cadence, interpretation, training, and service—not broader data access.
- Stage 3 must remain viable with report revenue set to zero; report value cannot finance away rights or evaluation independence.

### 4.10 Enterprise integration

- The lead enterprise architecture is a governed mixed coalition: embedded worker experience, enterprise-side source mapping, certified minimized exchange, independent proof and evaluation, periodic reconciliation, and shared correction and exit protocols.
- Platform-native full-stack control is rejected.
- Signed delivered events do not prove pre-transmission source completeness; a privacy-minimized eligible-source-population ledger or equivalent direct evidence remains necessary.
- Failed-proof privacy is tested across payloads, timing, callbacks, logs, CRM, loyalty, support, warehouses, traffic, and worker interfaces.
- Actual legal, vendor, cloud, key, administrator, support, backup, analytics, incident, subpoena, and exit control determines separation.
- Complete correction fan-out, capacity pause, production-shaped journey conformance, and exercised platform exit remain launch gates.

### 4.11 Operating economics and founder boundaries

- Stage 0 remains lightweight, data-free, promise-limited, and capable of pausing while Brent is unavailable.
- Stage 3 is a prepaid, capped, complete research-and-operation program including severe-but-plausible stress and post-pilot tail.
- The lead Stage 3 direction combines diversified partner and unrestricted support with a ring-fenced fiscal-sponsor or hosted administrative home that passes independence and exit review.
- Report-funded launch is rejected.
- Usable committed support, replacement-cost concentration, protected public goods, differentiated capacity caps, independent certification and appeal funding, liquid reserves, replaceable services, safe pricing, and bounded founder work remain mandatory.
- A separate OCA entity is formed only when funded duties require it.

### 4.12 Evaluation and claims

- Invitation or assignment is the preferred primary population estimand when identifiable and ethical; later-funnel estimands remain separately useful but cannot generalize upward.
- The complete denominator ladder is independently reconciled.
- Low subgroup power, outcome-dependent missingness, dependence, weak detection, and short follow-up create uncertainty or narrower claims—not fairness or safety clearance.
- The preregistered primary result leads public reporting.
- Workflow, construct, intervention, and portability claims are separate and non-inheriting.
- Protected rights are never withheld or randomized for methodological convenience.
- Reproducibility uses governed rerun and minimized analytical state rather than a public or permanent person-level dossier.
- One site yields a site-specific result; broader claims require claim-appropriate replication.

### 4.13 Distributed integrity

- The standard remains ledger-neutral; public blockchain is rejected as required or initial infrastructure.
- Signatures establish provenance, signed registries and federation establish current authority, credentials minimize disclosure, mutable systems carry active truth, and federated computation preserves source custody.
- Transparency services are limited to approved non-personal public institutional artifacts where the value of non-equivocation exceeds permanence and metadata cost.
- No identity evidence, worker response, conduct event, NCS, VCI, CQ, stable identifier, person-specific status or credential correlator, proof activity, permission, benefit, dispute, incident, remedy, recovery activity, or person-specific hash or commitment enters public immutable infrastructure.
- Cryptographic validity never replaces truth, fairness, attribution, source completeness, legal, challenge, correction, or evaluation gates.
- OCA does not become the mandatory blockchain, identity, wallet, credential, status, federation, transparency, or report-computation operator.

## 5. Surviving candidates open to empirical change

The following are lead choices, not constitutional truths:

- The NCS wording and four-direction response scale
- The provisional `+10, +3, −3, −10` points and weighted-average transformation
- The fulfilled-over-finalized simple VCI research calculation
- Exact event-class and responsibility profiles
- The consumer presentation of two dimensions, categories, explanations, proof eligibility, or a carefully tested combination
- Confidence and sufficiency labels
- Recency and legacy treatment within each evidence class
- Exact context-transfer coefficients after a mapping is independently authorized
- Proof validity periods, assurance steps, and status approach
- Exact non-positional benefit and discovery journey
- Credential, wallet, federation, cryptographic, status, and transparency profiles
- Report-family technologies, products, packaging, cadence, and pricing
- Enterprise adapters, platforms, service levels, and provider composition
- Pilot population, assignment, sample, duration, follow-up, partners, jurisdictions, and evaluator
- Stage-specific budgets, capacities, concentration thresholds, reserves, prices, and organization form

Research may reject any candidate. Rejection must return to the nearest surviving structural boundary rather than import a previously rejected architecture by convenience.

## 6. Rejected paths preserved at freeze

The following do not reenter parameter work as ordinary alternatives:

- Universal public consumer ranking, search, or profile
- An unrestricted combined CQ that permits component compensation
- Default nonzero context transfer or industry-label transfer
- Negative verifier disclosure, binary proof-failure response, or a nonpresentation marker
- Employee-requested CQ presentation or repeated coercive prompting
- Scarce, rights-related, punitive, or materially positional initial benefits
- Central cross-company raw behavioral or biometric dossier
- High identity assurance for every ordinary low-value proof
- Direct source retrieval that reveals a consumer's presentation activity
- Open-ended report or member query access
- Platform-native full-stack control
- Report-funded Stage 3 launch
- Treating founder time as elastic unpaid infrastructure
- Public blockchain as required or initial infrastructure
- Treating signatures, ledgers, or transparency receipts as proof of semantic truth or source completeness

Reopening a rejected path requires an explicit redesign record, not a favorable parameter result.

## 9. Change-control rule

Every proposed change is classified before implementation.

### Parameter change

A change to a surviving candidate inside frozen boundaries requires:

- Stated question and intended decision
- Preregistered comparison and failure conditions
- Synthetic or human evidence appropriate to the question
- Affected open questions and candidate decisions
- Versioned result and updated candidate status

### Structural amendment

A change to a constitutional, required-for-coherence, rejected, or scope boundary requires:

- Written reason the current architecture cannot satisfy the need
- Full dependency and affected-rights map
- Adversarial review from consumer, worker, business, technical, operational, governance, cultural, accessibility, legal, and evaluation perspectives as applicable
- Explicit alternatives, including retaining the current design
- New or amended simulation where the mechanism is structurally testable
- Decision-register amendment that preserves the prior decision
- New specification version and new freeze artifact
- Clear statement of whether prior results were visible when the amendment was proposed

No implementation vendor, pilot partner, funder, prospective report buyer, or founder preference may silently convert a parameter change into a structural amendment.

## 10. Architecture-freeze decision

**Decision: FREEZE ACCEPTED FOR EMPIRICAL PARAMETER AND COMPREHENSION RESEARCH.**

The surviving architecture is internally coherent enough to begin quantitative and human-facing research. This decision does not authorize Stage 3, real-person data, a real score, a credential, a benefit, a partner announcement, an OCA operating claim, a report product, or public marketing of validated outcomes.

The next highest-value work is to turn the surviving NCS, VCI, CQ, recency, confidence, and context-mapping candidates into a parameter-research program with explicit truth targets, synthetic scenarios, human-comprehension questions, and decisions each experiment is permitted to make.
