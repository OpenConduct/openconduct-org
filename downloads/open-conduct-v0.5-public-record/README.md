# Open Conduct v0.5 public record

**Prepared:** August 22, 2026

**Status:** Public-proposal package

**Canonical home:** https://openconduct.org/

This package contains the current working specification, decision register, open questions, architecture records, product contract, adversarial review, and synthetic research Suites A–L behind the Open Conduct proposal.

## Read this record responsibly

- Conduct is a proposal. No Conduct Quotient, operating network, alliance, certification, benefit program, or score-dispute system exists today.
- The simulations compare structures against declared failure conditions. They do not establish human validity, fairness, legality, security, business demand, cultural suitability, or production readiness.
- Each document retains its own status. Candidate and evidence-only material does not become an approved production rule because it appears in this archive.
- The readable component pages at openconduct.org remain the best starting point for most people.
