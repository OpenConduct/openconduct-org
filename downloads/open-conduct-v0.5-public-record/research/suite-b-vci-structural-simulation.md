# Conduct Ecosystem — Suite B VCI Structural Simulation Report v0.1

## The answer in human terms

The VCI idea survives, but only in its stricter form.

Verified operational evidence can add information beyond transaction count, commercial value, and digital access in the synthetic model. The best architecture is not a naked business-generated score. It is:

1. A complete, reciprocal responsibility ledger that records the opportunity, outcome, business failures, exceptions, disputes, corrections, attribution, and missingness.
2. A simple fulfilled-over-finalized calculation that may be used internally only when that ledger clears its structural gates.
3. A full event-class and evidence-quality profile that always remains behind the calculation.
4. A purpose-specific proof, if later justified, produced only from approved event classes and valid evidence.
5. No portable VCI whenever the ledger cannot establish completeness, causation, attribution, reviewability, or correction.

This preserves the original role of VCI as the rational, data-backed balance to NCS. It also sharpens what “verified” must mean: not merely that a business system produced the record, but that the responsibility, source universe, attribution, causation, evidence, and lifecycle are fit for the claimed use.

## 1. What was tested

Suite B compared:

| ID | Candidate | Role in the comparison |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | Fulfilled-over-finalized ratio | Simple provisional calculation |
| B2 | Opportunity-balanced ratio | Uses pre-outcome registered responsibilities as the denominator |
| B3 | Event-class profile | Preserves separate classes, coverage, exceptions, and reliability without requiring one universal value |
| B4 | Reliability model | Advanced statistical challenger with uncertainty |
| B5 | Purpose-bound VCI proof | Produces only a decision needed for one approved purpose |
| B6 | No portable VCI | Required null architecture |

The full run included:

- 17 Suite B scenario-and-mode groups
- 623 unique common synthetic histories
- 1,200 synthetic consumers per history
- 3,738 candidate-case evaluations
- Source incompleteness, selective registration, selective delivery, easy-positive flooding, business-failure omission, control error, disputes, failed correction, transaction-volume differences, and actor misattribution
- Identical generated history for every candidate within each comparison

The preserved result package is `simulation/run_packages/suite-b-v0.1-results.json`.

## 2. The simple ratio remains useful—but only after admissibility

Under the 45 histories that cleared all four structural gate families, B1 had the strongest relationship to the clean event-level responsibility record among the simple candidates:

| Candidate | Correlation with generated operational propensity | Correlation with clean eligible event rate | Median error from clean representation | Transaction-volume correlation |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| B1: Fulfilled over finalized | 0.505 | **0.935** | 0.044 | 0.037 |
| B2: Opportunity balanced | 0.512 | 0.894 | 0.065 | 0.055 |
| B3: Event-class profile, derived material-class summary | 0.497 | 0.911 | **0.040** | **0.001** |
| B4: Simplified reliability challenger | **0.548** | 0.903 | 0.034 | 0.090 |
| B5: Generic purpose state | 0.338 | 0.660 | Not comparable as a binary threshold | −0.210 |

The useful conclusion is not that 0.935 predicts real-world performance. The synthetic generator defines the test signal. The comparative conclusion is that a fulfilled-over-finalized calculation describes eligible event outcomes well when—and only when—the underlying evidence is legitimate and complete.

B1 therefore remains the provisional simple research calculation. It is not a standalone portable product, production formula, or substitute for the evidence profile.

## 3. Opportunity coverage belongs in evidence quality, not the consumer score

B2 was intended to resist selective issuance by dividing fulfilled events by responsibilities registered before the result was known. The principle is essential. The calculation is not.

When source delivery was only 40%, B2's median error from the clean representation rose to 0.363, compared with 0.217 for B1. B2 treated registered-but-undelivered events as if they were consumer nonfulfillment. That violates the rule that missing or unresolved business-system data is unknown, not negative conduct.

Selective registration also defeated the denominator. When generated outcome-aware registration reached an odds ratio of 10, median intended-signal recovery fell to:

- B1: 0.328
- B2: 0.293
- B3 derived summary: 0.322

The right repair is architectural:

- Registration coverage, delivery coverage, lifecycle closure, and source reconciliation remain mandatory.
- They determine whether the VCI evidence is usable and how confidently it can be described.
- They do not become missing negative events in the consumer's numerator or denominator.
- A business that cannot reconcile its eligible source population cannot issue the affected portable VCI evidence.

B2 should therefore be rejected as a consumer-standing formula while its opportunity ledger becomes a required evidence-quality and conformance control.

## 4. The event-class profile is mandatory

B3 was the most stable simple representation across the broad adversarial matrix. Its median normalized error from its clean counterpart was 0.085, compared with 0.111 for B1 and 0.127 for B2. Its transaction-volume correlation remained near zero.

Its value is not the derived scalar used for numerical comparison. Its value is preserving differences that one number hides:

- Routine fulfillment versus material responsibilities
- Fulfilled, pending, disputed, finalized-failure, business-caused, external-cause, waived, corrected, reversed, invalidated, and unknown states
- Registration, delivery, closure, and correction coverage
- Evidence and attribution confidence
- Issuer and source concentration
- Event-class severity and evidentiary standard
- Exceptions, accommodations, and business obligations

The easy-event stress also shows why class separation should not mean simply discarding routine fulfillment. Under the highest generated easy-positive multiplier, the material-only summary lost substantial signal because too much positive evidence had been removed. The full profile should retain routine evidence with caps or diminishing influence while preventing it from overwhelming material classes.

The resulting decision mirrors Suite A: a simple internal calculation may be useful, but it remains inseparable from the richer backing representation.

## 5. The advanced reliability model remains promising but unproven

B4 produced the strongest correlation with the generator's latent operational propensity and the lowest median numerical distortion. Shrinkage helped stabilize sparse histories.

But Suite B v0.1 could implement only a simplified beta-smoothed approximation. The base generator has one issuer layer and no full source hierarchy, so it cannot test the candidate described in the design package: a contextual reliability model with issuer, event, source, and uncertainty structure. The simplified version also retained a higher relationship with transaction volume than B1 or B3.

B4 therefore does not win. It remains an advanced challenger that must earn its complexity in a later multi-issuer, multi-source comparison. It may improve estimation, but it cannot determine whether the underlying events are legitimate or repair a failed ledger.

## 6. The generic purpose-proof rule fails

B5's generic test implementation qualified a consumer after at least one relevant positive event and no relevant adverse event. That rule lost too much information and developed a median transaction-volume correlation of −0.228. More activity created more chances to encounter an adverse event, turning continued participation into a potential eligibility treadmill.

This does not reject purpose-bound VCI proofs. It rejects a universal “one positive and no negatives” shortcut.

Any later purpose proof must define:

- The exact event classes relevant to that purpose
- Sufficient but non-purchasable evidence
- Caps and clustering for repeated routine events
- Treatment of sparse, pending, disputed, corrected, and unknown histories
- A rule that does not reward manufactured activity or punish ordinary continued participation
- Independent justification of any threshold and its practical consequences

## 7. Source integrity dominates formula choice

### Incomplete registration and delivery degrade every representation

As generated registration completeness fell from 1.0 to 0.4, median intended-signal recovery fell from approximately 0.49–0.50 to 0.29–0.36 across B1–B3. Median distortion from the clean representation more than doubled.

At 40% delivery, B1 and B3 retained median intended correlations near 0.38, while B2 fell to 0.252 because it converted missing delivery into apparent consumer failure.

### Actor misattribution destroys meaning

At a generated attribution-error rate of 0.6, intended-signal recovery fell to approximately 0.19–0.22 across B1–B4. Median numerical distortion reached approximately 0.16–0.30 depending on the representation.

No formula can decide whether a reservation holder, payer, traveler, guest, caregiver, delegate, account owner, or service recipient was the person responsible. Ambiguity must suppress or narrow the event before calculation.

### Easy positive events can manufacture standing

At the highest generated easy-positive stress, transaction-volume correlation rose to approximately:

- B1: 0.157
- B2: 0.229
- B4 simplified challenger: 0.163
- B3 material-class summary: 0.018

This supports event-class caps, clustering, diminishing routine weight, and evidence-diversity requirements. The mere existence of many transactions cannot become conduct status.

### Business and control errors create unequal false adversity

Across the adversarial matrix, the median generated false-adverse rate was 4.9%. The median gap affecting low-resource consumers was 6.1 percentage points and the 90th-percentile gap was 8.7 points.

These are not prevalence estimates. They show the direction of the risk: when businesses omit their own failures or misclassify what the consumer could control, operational data can reproduce resource and access inequality while appearing objective.

## 8. The gates are part of VCI, not implementation detail

The adversarial design deliberately created many invalid ledgers. In 578 of 623 histories, a naked B1, B2, or B4 scalar still produced an output while at least one source, reciprocity, dispute/correction, or attribution gate failed.

That count describes stress-test coverage, not expected real-world prevalence. The categorical finding is what matters: **a formula that continues producing a portable result after its evidence fails is not a valid VCI architecture.**

The candidate architecture must suppress, narrow, or mark unavailable any result when:

- Eligible responsibilities cannot be reconciled to the complete source population
- Registration or delivery was selected after the outcome
- Business failures, exceptions, accommodations, or external causes are missing
- A disputed or high-severity event lacks required finalization
- Correction and reversal have not reached every active effect
- The responsible actor cannot be established
- Routine volume overwhelms event diversity or materiality

## 9. Candidate verdicts

| Candidate | Suite B verdict | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| B1: Fulfilled over finalized | **Provisional lead simple research calculation** | Best simple description of clean eligible event outcomes; valid only behind non-compensating evidence gates and a complete profile |
| B2: Opportunity-balanced ratio | **Reject as a consumer score; retain the opportunity ledger as a gate** | Converts missing delivery into consumer failure and cannot cure selective registration |
| B3: Event-class profile | **Required backing representation** | Preserves lifecycle, class, coverage, causation, uncertainty, and volume limits that a scalar hides |
| B4: Reliability model | **Retain as an advanced challenger** | Sparse-history stabilization is promising, but the full multi-issuer model was not instantiated and complexity cannot repair evidence |
| B5: Purpose-bound proof | **Retain the output category; reject the generic rule** | Purpose limitation is desirable, but one-positive/no-adverse creates information loss and a participation treadmill |
| B6: No portable VCI | **Retain as the constitutional null** | Must win whenever the ledger cannot clear its source, legitimacy, rights, attribution, correction, or incremental-value gates |

No candidate is approved for real-person use or a production benefit.

## 10. What this run could not establish

Suite B v0.1 intentionally records several limits:

- The full B4 hierarchical reliability model requires multiple issuers, sources, event classes, and policy regimes.
- Issuer concentration was carried through the design but not instantiated by the single-issuer base generator.
- Correction, latency, and rights-capacity factors exposed declared risk and stale-effect proxies but did not execute the complete PSB-006 state machines inside this numerical runner.
- The nonresponse proxy does not separately model accessible notice, delivery failure, cure, and affirmative admission.
- Synthetic incremental value cannot establish that real VCI data will add value beyond existing transaction, loyalty, fraud, or commercial-risk systems.
- Responsibility legitimacy, proportionality, accessibility, legal validity, consumer comprehension, and independent finalization remain human and institutional judgments.

These limits constrain the claim; they do not reverse the structural decisions above.

## 11. Decision and next step

VCI should remain a core part of CQ. Suite B strengthens the original two-part concept: NCS provides the human record, while VCI supplies a distinct operational record that may corroborate, contradict, or contextualize it.

The operational record should be represented as a governed event and evidence profile. A fulfilled-over-finalized calculation may remain as the provisional simple internal summary, but only after the ledger clears non-compensating gates. Opportunity coverage becomes an admissibility and confidence control, not a way to charge missing business data against the consumer.

The next structural comparison is Suite C: whether NCS and VCI should remain visibly separate, form a conjunctive tier, support only a purpose-bound proof, or enter any combined CQ representation at all.
