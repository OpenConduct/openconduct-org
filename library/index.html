HOW LABELS WORK

What each label tells you, and what it does not.

Current

Controls or accurately describes the present proposal.

Candidate

A leading method, parameter, or direction that still needs evidence.

Evidence-only

Informs the proposal without controlling it or proving field outcomes.

Historical

Preserves an earlier state or argument that later work changed.

Superseded

Replaced by a named later artifact and retained for traceability.

STRUCTURAL SIMULATION

Twelve suites narrowed the architecture.

They expose contradictions and compare structures against known failures. They do not establish human validity, legal approval, field fairness, business demand, security, or production readiness.

A

NCS structure

Preserve response distribution, sufficiency, and uncertainty.

B

VCI structure

Separate responsibility, evidence, lifecycle, and business failure.

C

CQ synthesis

Reject unconstrained averaging and preserve non-compensation.

D

Context transfer

Start mappings at zero and require exact source–target–purpose profiles.

E

Benefit result

Use consumer-initiated, purpose-bound results and genuinely additional benefits.

F

Identity and custody

Minimize assurance and avoid central raw-history custody.

G

Network reports

Use fixed, privacy-protected aggregate analysis instead of open queries.

H

Enterprise integration

Preserve roles, provenance, tenant boundaries, correction, and safe failure.

I

Operating independence

Separate roles, preserve exit, and avoid report-funded launch dependency.

J

Evaluation and claims

Measure the full funnel and constrain public claims.

K

CQ display

Advance capped synthesis and the working 1.00–5.00 display for research.

L

CQ scope

Begin with company-specific CQ and keep overall CQ in bounded research.
