# Net Conduct Score

Net Conduct Score (NCS) is the proposed open measure of human experience in an eligible, verified interaction.

The bounded question is: **Based only on how this customer interacted with people, would you want to serve them again?**

The current draft uses four directional responses: Definitely yes (+10), Probably yes (+3), Probably no (-3), and Definitely no (-10). It has no midpoint and includes an abstention option when someone cannot or should not answer.

NCS does not measure whether a customer is a good person, how much they spent, whether they complained, whether they accepted a bad outcome, or whether an employee personally liked them.

Read the complete page: https://openconduct.org/netconductscore
